**Visual:** one real output from the product — a satellite scene with a grounded
box and the confidence chip visible — set at low opacity behind the title, or as
a clean panel on the right third. Use an actual screenshot from the testing-corpus
gallery. **Do not use stock imagery or clip art**; a real screenshot signals a
real system before a word is read.

**Palette:** dark instrument panel, single accent. It should look like ground-segment
software, not a startup landing page.
